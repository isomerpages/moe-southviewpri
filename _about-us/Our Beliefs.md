<h4><strong><u>Vision</u></strong></h4>
<p><strong>A School of Passionate Learners with a Heart to Serve.</strong>
</p>
<p>The school vision is one where we aspire to bring out the personal best
in everyone in all endeavours. We want to build a culture of learning where
staff and pupils are encouraged to deepen their learning and be anchored
on values.</p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 50%;" height="auto" width="100%" src="/images/OB1.jpg">
</div>
<h4><strong><u>Mission</u></strong></h4>
<p><strong>Inspiring Minds, Moulding Character.</strong>
</p>
<p>The school will do our utmost to inspire our pupils by focusing on pupils’
needs, learning aptitudes and starting point so as to increase their motivation
to learn and build positive attitudes towards learning and be lifelong
learners. We are also committed to nurturing a future generation of useful
and responsible citizens by instilling in them a sense of duty, a sense
of gratitude and to serve with humility.</p>
<h4><strong><u>Motto</u></strong></h4>
<p><strong>With Heart and Mind.</strong>
</p>
<p>Our school motto supports the school’s Vision and Mission by encouraging
staff and pupils to learn, understand and practise with an open and discerning
mind and to serve with a gracious heart so as to contribute to society.</p>

<h4><strong><u>Total Curriculum Framework (TCF)</u></strong></h4>
<p></p>
<div class="isomer-image-wrapper">
<img style="width: 80%;" height="auto" width="100%" alt="" src="/images/TCF.jpg">
</div>
<p><strong>Nurturing Every Child: From Potential to Excellence</strong>
</p>
<p>Welcome to the overview of South View Primary School’s <strong>Total Curriculum Framework (TCF)</strong>.
This informational guide outlines our holistic blueprint for education,
explaining how our school community collaborates to support, grow and transform
our students.</p>
<p><strong>What is the Total Curriculum Framework (TCF)?</strong>
</p>
<p>The TCF encapsulates SVPS’s comprehensive, student-centric approach to
teaching and learning. It is designed to look beyond mere academic milestones,
focussing instead on developing well-rounded, future-ready children.</p>
<p>Our student journey is best symbolized by the transformation of the <strong>South View Diamond</strong>:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>The Unpolished Gem:</strong> Represents our students when they
first step through our doors—new, unpolished and teeming with raw potential.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>The Polished Diamond:</strong> Represents our goal for every student
by the time they graduate in Primary 6—a sparkling polished diamond who
is confident and ready to embrace the next chapter of their educational
journey.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>The TCF is the framework our community uses to polish that gem and help
every student achieve their personal best.</p>
<p><strong>The Core Pillars: Why, What and How</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>1. WHY: Our Core Purpose (The Centre)</strong>
</p>
<p>At the heart of the TCF is our School Vision and Desired Student Outcome
(DSO): <strong>To develop Passionate Learners with a Heart to Serve</strong>.
Everything we do is driven by the purpose of nurturing a whole child who
loves learning and desires to contribute meaningfully to society.</p>
<p><strong>2. WHAT: The Three Guiding Principles</strong>
</p>
<p>To keep our curriculum aligned, effective, and impactful, everything we
teach is grounded in <strong>three Guiding Principles</strong>:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Student-Centricity:</strong> We place our students at the centre,
adapting to their diverse interests, learning needs, backgrounds, and voices.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Intentionality:</strong> School is never random. Every lesson,
project, and activity is purposely designed to intentionally build specific
life skills, values, and long-term dispositions.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Coherence:</strong> We connect the dots across subjects. Learning
does not happen in silos. We intentionally help students make connections
across disciplines, apply their learning in different contexts, and see
how knowledge and skills come together to solve authentic problems. In
doing so, they develop deeper understanding, greater confidence and a stronger
sense of purpose in their learning.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>3. HOW: The Four Enablers</strong>
</p>
<p>We turn this framework into a lived reality through four foundational
pillars:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Skilful Educators:</strong> Teachers who constantly refine their
craft to deliver high-quality, professional guidance.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Student-Centred &amp; Holistic Curriculum:</strong> Tailored learning
pathways that accommodate varying strengths and interests.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Caring &amp; Enabling Environment:</strong> Safe, inclusive, and
relationship-driven spaces that promote well-being of the students.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Meaningful Partnership:</strong> Strategic networks linking the
school, parents, and community together to enrich the overall ecosystem.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>How Stakeholders Use the TCF to Guide Students</strong>
</p>
<p>Nurturing a child requires a collective ecosystem. Here is briefly how
our three primary stakeholder groups utilise the TCF as a practical guide:</p>
<p><strong>How the School Uses the TCF</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Intentional Design:</strong> School leaders and teachers use the
framework as a constant checklist during lesson and programme planning,
ensuring every curriculum piece has a clear "Why" and clear learning outcomes.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Differentiated Support:</strong> The school designs varied tiered
interventions so that students receive customised attention based on whether
they require core instruction, moderate assistance, or specialised stretching.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Creating the Space:</strong> Educators utilize the enablers to
cultivate a caring environment, opening up platforms where student feedback
and voice actively shape the daily classroom experience.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>&nbsp;How Students Use the TCF</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Taking Ownership:</strong> Students look at the framework as their
checklist for personal growth. It empowers them to speak up, exercise choice,
and take responsibility for their own education.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Building a Growth Mindset:</strong> Students understand that challenges
are an intentional part of their polishing process. They learn to embrace
the traits of a "Passionate Learner"—asking questions, sharing ideas, and
displaying resilience when work gets hard.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Connecting to the World:</strong> It encourages students to see
the big picture, understanding how their lessons in school directly relate
to real-world issues and their desire to serve.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>How Parents Use the TCF</strong>
</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p><strong>Extending Learning to the Home:</strong> A strong home-school partnership
is vital. Parents use the principles of <em>Coherence</em> and <em>Intentionality</em> to
reinforce school values during daily home conversations, helping children
apply classroom learning to real-life situations.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Supporting Holistic Strengths:</strong> Guided by <em>Student-Centricity</em>,
parents partner with the school to celebrate their child's unique character
traits, passions, and personal bests, rather than focussing solely on academic
scores.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p><strong>Active Collaboration:</strong> Parents partner closely with us.
By connecting regularly with teachers and joining school initiatives, they
help create a consistent, supportive environment for their child at school
and at home.</p>
</li>
</ul>
<p>Let us work hand-in-hand to bring the best out of our children, moving
them from raw potential into sparkling, compassionate contributors to our
world.</p>
